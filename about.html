Our bakery, our neighborhood

Our story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood gathering place built around fresh baking and the pleasure of sharing food. We welcome regulars stopping in on their commute and families planning their next celebration.

How we choose ingredients

Our goal is to choose quality ingredients and seasonal flavors when possible. Ask our team about ingredients and allergens for any specific item.

The people behind the counter

Our bakers prepare breads and pastries; our front counter team helps neighbors find something for breakfast, a gift, or an event. Individual staff profiles can be added when the bakery supplies them.
